Frequently asked questions

Does the EAA apply to my business?

It applies to consumer-facing e-commerce, banking, transport, telecom and e-book services in the EU. Microenterprises (fewer than 10 employees and at most €2 million turnover) are generally exempt from the service requirements. If you're unsure, send me your URL and I'll tell you what I'd check.

Won't an accessibility overlay widget solve this?

No. Overlay widgets don't fix the underlying code, and accessibility experts and regulators don't accept them as compliance. Real fixes happen in your theme and templates.

Is automated testing enough?

Automated tools reliably catch about a third of WCAG issues. That's why the Full Audit also includes manual keyboard and screen-reader testing of your most important user journeys.

Is this legal advice?

No. It's a technical assessment against WCAG 2.2 AA and EN 301 549. For legal questions, please consult a lawyer.

Which platforms do you work with?

Any public website can be audited. For fixes I work with Shopify themes, WordPress / WooCommerce, and custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
