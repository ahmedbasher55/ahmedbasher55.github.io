UAE e-invoicing (PINT AE) from your Excel or ERP, without replacing your system

From July 2027 every VAT-registered business in the UAE must exchange structured e-invoices through an Accredited Service Provider (ASP). The ASP handles Peppol transport and FTA reporting, but your invoice data still has to arrive as valid PINT AE XML. I build that bridge: Excel, Odoo, QuickBooks, Zoho exports or a custom database → PINT AE invoices and credit notes that pass the official rules.

The gap

Many ERPs and spreadsheets used by SMEs can't produce PINT AE yet. The data mapping (TRN, emirate, VAT categories, transaction flags) is where projects stall.

Ministerial Decisions 244/2025 and 66/2026. Always confirm your dates with your tax adviser and the Ministry of Finance.
