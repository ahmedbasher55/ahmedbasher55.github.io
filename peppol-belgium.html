The Belgian B2B mandate

Live since 1 January 2026

Structured e-invoices are mandatory between Belgian VAT-registered businesses, exchanged over Peppol in the Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 format.

Peppol by default

The legal default is the Peppol network and the Peppol BIS format. PDF-by-email no longer meets the obligation for B2B invoices in scope.

The gap

Many SMEs still invoice from Excel or older software that can't produce Peppol BIS. Mapping the data to a valid file is where it stalls.

Belgian law of 6 February 2024 on structured electronic invoicing. Always confirm your situation with your accountant and the FPS Finance (SPF Finances / FOD Financiën).
